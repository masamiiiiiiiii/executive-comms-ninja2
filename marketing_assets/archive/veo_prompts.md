# Veo Prompts for Vertical Shorts

These prompts are designed to be used with Google Veo (or similar high-end AI video generators like Sora) to produce 6-second, portrait-oriented (9:16) videos that match the "Executive Comms Ninja" brand identity (beige background, sage green accents, vintage editorial/New Yorker aesthetic).

---

## Pattern 1: $10,000 (The Cost)
**Concept**: A price tag with the amount $10,000 being aggressively crossed out, followed by a punchy text reveal.

**Veo Generation Prompt:**
```text
A 6-second vertical (9:16) motion graphics animation. The background is a flat, warm beige (#efebdf). In the center of the frame, a highly detailed, vintage-style paper price tag hangs from a thin white string. The price tag is slightly off-white with subtle paper texture. Printed on the tag in elegant, high-contrast Playfair Display serif font is the text "$10,000". At the 2-second mark, a sudden, sharp, diagonal black line violently slashes through the number "$10,000", matching a jarring tempo. Immediately after the slash, the price tag rapidly drops out of frame. The text "Redefine the cost." fades in smoothly at the bottom of the screen in a deep charcoal serif font. The pacing is tense and rhythmic, like a dissonant jazz beat. The visual style is a minimalist, flat vector illustration reminiscent of a New Yorker magazine cover. No complex shadows or 3D effects. High quality, crisp lines.
```

---

## Pattern 2: Observation Over Intuition (The False Comfort)
**Concept**: A poised female executive looking at a tablet, but the data she seeks is visually unstable, illustrating the danger of intuition over hard numbers.

**Veo Generation Prompt:**
```text
A 6-second vertical (9:16) motion graphics animation. The background is a flat, warm beige (#efebdf). In the center is a half-body illustration of a sophisticated female corporate executive wearing a sage green (#8fa891) tailored blazer over a white shirt, her hair pulled back neatly. Her facial expression is calm and composed. She is holding and pointing at a modern digital tablet. On the tablet screen, a green data chart is displayed. At the 2-second mark, the chart on her tablet screen suddenly starts glitching and fluctuating erratically, breaking her polished facade. To her right, the text "OBSERVATION OVER INTUITION." appears in a large, elegant Playfair Display serif font in sage green, with "AI-Driven Insights." written underneath in a smaller, dark charcoal font. The animation is smooth but the screen glitch is abrupt. The art style is minimalist, flat vector illustration with muted colors and a refined, editorial aesthetic like the New Yorker. No photorealism.
```

## Pattern 3: Data From Dialog (The Viral Nightmare)
**Concept**: An abstract representation of audio waves turning into cold, hard data, symbolizing the extraction of a stutter or PR mistake.

**Veo Generation Prompt:**
```text
A 6-second vertical (9:16) motion graphics animation. The background is a flat, warm beige (#efebdf). In the center is a large, minimalist "Play" button icon (a right-pointing triangle) outlined in dark charcoal. The inside of the triangle is filled with a soft sage green (#8fa891). Inside the green triangle, an animated, abstract audio waveform representation plays out, composed of vertical lines and data blocks shifting rapidly, resembling digital noise or speech patterns being analyzed. The waveform moves erratically, not smoothly, reflecting an unsettling, dissonant rhythm. Below the icon, the text "Data From Dialog." is elegantly typeset in a large, dark sage green Playfair Display serif font. The overall motion should feel slightly syncopated and jarring, matching the tension of a PR crisis. The visual style is a minimalist, flat vector illustration with a classy, editorial aesthetic. High contrast and clean geometry.
```
